## 🆘 Common Questions

### Q: What if I don't get Google Business Profile access?
**A:** Follow up with the business owner. You may need:
- Different Google account email
- Different permission level (Manager vs Owner)
- Business verification first

### Q: What should I focus on while waiting for GBP access?
**A:**
1. Collect photos (100+)
2. Set up Google Search Console
3. Claim Yelp & TripAdvisor
4. Train staff on review requests
5. Create content calendar

### Q: How much time will this take per week?
**A:**
- Week 1: 8-10 hours (setup heavy)
- Week 2-4: 5-7 hours (if GBP access)
- Ongoing: 3-5 hours per week (maintenance)

### Q: What if I can't upload that many photos?
**A:** Quality > quantity, but quantity matters for SEO.
- Minimum: 10 photos/week
- Good: 20 photos/week
- Great: 30+ photos/week

Take photos during events, ask staff to help, use phone camera (it's fine!).

### Q: How long until we see results?
**A:**
- Week 1-2: Setup, minimal ranking changes
- Week 3-4: Start appearing occasionally in local pack
- Month 2: Noticeable improvement in visibility
- Month 3: Consistent local pack appearances
- Month 6: Dominating local searches

SEO takes 3-6 months. Stay consistent!

### Q: What's the most important thing to focus on?
**A:**
1. Google Business Profile optimization (40% of success)
2. Getting reviews (25% of success)
3. NAP consistency (already done!) (15% of success)

Focus your energy on #1 and #2.

### Q: Should I hire an SEO agency?
**A:** You can do this yourself with these guides! Save money by doing it in-house.

Only hire if:
- You truly don't have 3-5 hours/week
- You need technical SEO help (site is broken)
- You want to scale faster (they have tools/expertise)

Cost: $1,000-3,000/month for local SEO agency.
