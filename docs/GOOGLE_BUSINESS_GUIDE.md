## 🚨 Red Flags & Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Profile Suspended:**
- Usually policy violation
- Contact Google Business support: 1-844-491-9665
- Review Google's guidelines
- May need to verify business again

**Reviews Removed:**
- Google removes spam/fake reviews
- Don't panic if legit reviews removed (appeal)
- Never write fake reviews

**Sudden Ranking Drop:**
- Check for NAP inconsistencies
- Verify profile is still verified
- Check for new competitors
- Review recent changes you made
- Usually recovers in 1-2 weeks

**Photos Rejected:**
- Usually quality/content issues
- Review photo guidelines
- Re-upload higher quality versions

**Can't Edit Info:**
- Verify you have manager/owner access
- May be in pending verification state
- Contact support if issues persist

### Getting Help

**Google Business Profile Support:**
- Phone: 1-844-491-9665 (US)
- Twitter: @GoogleMyBiz
- Help Center: https://support.google.com/business
