## 🚨 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Inconsistent NAP** - Use EXACT same phone/address everywhere
2. **Ignoring reviews** - Respond to ALL reviews within 24 hours
3. **Poor quality photos** - Only upload high-quality, well-lit photos
4. **Giving up too soon** - SEO takes 3-6 months minimum
5. **Sporadic posting** - Consistency beats intensity
6. **Keyword stuffing** - Write naturally for humans
7. **Fake reviews** - NEVER. Google will penalize you
8. **Not tracking** - Can't improve what you don't measure
9. **Forgetting mobile** - Most searches are mobile
10. **Being too promotional** - Mix educational/entertaining content
