## 📈 How to Check Your Metrics

### Google Business Profile Insights

**Where:** Google Business Profile dashboard → Insights

**What you'll see:**
- How customers search for your business
- Where customers view your business on Google
- Customer actions
- Phone calls
- Direction requests
- Photos

**How often to check:** Weekly

**Export data:** Download monthly for records

---

### Search Rankings

**Manual Method:**

1. Use incognito/private browser window
2. Clear location to Salt Lake City or use location spoofer
3. Search for keyword
4. Note your position
5. Check if you're in local pack (top 3 map results)

**Keywords to track:**
- "bar near me"
- "bars in salt lake city"
- "piano bar salt lake city"
- "downtown slc nightlife"
- "live music salt lake city"
- "bars near gateway slc"
- "bars near vivint arena"

**Tools to help:**
- Local Falcon (paid, very accurate)
- BrightLocal (paid)
- Google Search Console (free, less specific)

**How often to check:** Monthly (rankings fluctuate daily)

---

## 🚦 Traffic Light System

Use this simple system to know if you're on track:

### 🟢 Green (On Track):
- Map views increasing 20%+ monthly
- 10+ new reviews per month
- 100% review response rate
- 10+ photos uploaded weekly
- 2+ Google Posts per week
- Appearing in local pack occasionally

### 🟡 Yellow (Needs Attention):
- Map views flat or slightly down
- 5-9 new reviews per month
- 90-99% review response rate
- 5-9 photos uploaded weekly
- 1 Google Post per week
- Not appearing in local pack

### 🔴 Red (Critical):
- Map views declining
- <5 new reviews per month
- <90% review response rate
- <5 photos uploaded weekly
- No Google Posts in 2+ weeks
- Never appearing in local pack
- New negative reviews

**If you're in yellow or red:**
- Review what changed
- Check competitor activity
- Increase content frequency
- Focus on reviews
- Double down on photos

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Track consistently** - Same day, same time every week
2. **Export data** - Download reports monthly (GBP can delete old data)
3. **Look for trends** - Single-week fluctuations are normal
4. **Compare to competitors** - Are they doing something new?
5. **Celebrate progress** - Even small wins matter
6. **Stay patient** - SEO takes 3-6 months to really work
7. **Adjust strategy** - If something isn't working after a month, change it
8. **Document everything** - Future you will thank you
9. **Share wins with team** - Get everyone invested
10. **Keep photos of milestones** - 100 reviews, 1000 map views, etc.

---

## 🔄 Quarterly Review

**Every 3 months, do a deep dive:**

1. **Compare to baseline** (Month 1 data)
2. **Review all KPIs** - On track for 6-month goals?
3. **Competitive analysis** - Where do you stand?
4. **Strategy adjustment** - What's working? What's not?
5. **Resource allocation** - Need more photos? More posts?
6. **Set next quarter goals** - Be specific and measurable
